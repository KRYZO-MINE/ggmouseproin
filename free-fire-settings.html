Free Fire keyboard and mouse setup

Build a Free Fire keymap, align it with your HUD, and troubleshoot controls with this GG Mouse Pro 3 setup guide.

Download & set upGet support

Before you start

Keep a copy of your current Free Fire HUD before changing controls. These are suggested bindings, not an official preset or a guarantee of game compatibility. Check the game’s current rules on external input and third-party mapping before using them.

Connect and activate

  1. Install GG Mouse Pro 3 and connect your keyboard and mouse.
  2. Confirm typing and pointer movement work in Android before opening the game.
  3. Complete the activation method available on your device.
  4. Open your game profile in GG Mouse Pro and align the mapping controls with your current HUD. Save the profile before testing.

Suggested keymap and customization

Use these as starting points and assign each key to the matching on-screen control. Existing Free Fire keymap templates can help you compare layouts.

Test movement, camera control, and individual buttons separately in a permitted practice mode. Moving or resizing the HUD means you must realign your map.

Sensitivity and aim

Keep mouse DPI fixed while testing. Adjust one setting at a time and compare small target movements with wider turns. There is no universal best sensitivity: screen size, DPI, and the game’s own settings change the result. See the sensitivity guide for a repeatable calibration process.

When a key misses its target

Check screen orientation, HUD scale, and whether the intended profile is active. If every key stops responding, check activation before moving individual bindings. For connection or permission failures, use the troubleshooting checklist.
