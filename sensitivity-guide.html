Calibrate mouse sensitivity on Android

Use a repeatable process to tune mouse DPI, camera movement, and scoped sensitivity without relying on universal presets.

Download & set upGet support

Establish a baseline

Record your mouse DPI, app sensitivity, game sensitivity, and screen orientation. Keep the mouse surface and available movement space consistent. Save your current profile before making changes.

Change one variable at a time

  1. Keep DPI fixed and choose a repeatable mouse movement.
  2. Observe how far the camera turns in a permitted practice mode.
  3. Adjust one sensitivity control slightly.
  4. Repeat the same movement and record the result.
  5. After broad turns feel comfortable, test small adjustments and scoped movement separately.

Separate sensitivity from connection problems

Overshooting a target can call for a lower setting. Missing input, disconnects, and pauses require a connection check. Changing sensitivity will not repair an unstable adapter or expired activation.

Save settings per layout

Record the final values with the game profile. Recheck after HUD or display changes. Return to the Free Fire or PUBG guide to verify the rest of the bindings.
